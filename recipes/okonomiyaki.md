# Okonomiyaki

## Ingredients
- Cabbage (shredded)
- Eggs
- Flour (or okonomiyaki flour)
- Dashi stock (or water)
- Green onions
- Protein of choice (pork belly, shrimp, or squid — adjust macros accordingly)
- Okonomiyaki sauce
- Kewpie mayonnaise
- Bonito flakes (optional)
- Aonori (seaweed flakes, optional)
- Pickled ginger (optional)

## Macros
- **Calories:** 600
- **Carbs:** 50g
- **Protein:** 45g
- **Fat:** 10g

## Notes
- Kane's dinner staple. Japanese savory pancake/pizza hybrid.
- Cabbage-heavy base makes it filling without being calorie-dense.
- Protein source (pork, shrimp, squid) drives the 45g protein.
- Sauce and mayo add flavor — use light hand to keep fat at 10g.
- Highly customizable — add whatever protein/veg you have.

## Instructions
1. Mix shredded cabbage, flour, eggs, dashi, and green onions into batter
2. Add protein and mix in
3. Pour onto hot oiled pan, shape into thick pancake
4. Cook 4-5 min per side until golden and crispy
5. Flip (use two spatulas or a confident wrist)
6. Top with okonomiyaki sauce, mayo, bonito flakes, aonori
7. Serve immediately — cut into wedges
