# Morning Smoothie

## Ingredients
- 1 cup frozen blueberries
- 1 cup fat free Fairlife milk
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp hemp seeds
- 1 tbsp organic banana powder
- 1/2 tbsp flax seeds
- **1 scoop Dymatize ISO100 Hydrolyzed Vanilla Whey Protein** *(added 2026-06-02)*
- **1 scoop Klean Athlete Creatine** *(added 2026-06-02)*
- **1 Bloom Energy stick — Raspberry Lemon flavor** *(added 2026-06-02)*

## Macros (with additions)
- **Calories:** ~445 *(Bloom energy stick caloric impact minimal ~5-10)*
- **Carbs:** ~47g
- **Protein:** ~37g
- **Fat:** ~13g

## Macros (original, without protein/creatine)
- **Calories:** ~335
- **Carbs:** 45g
- **Protein:** 12g
- **Fat:** 12g

## Notes
- Kane's standard breakfast smoothie — now enhanced with post-workout protein and creatine since switching to morning Tonal (2026-06-02).
- Bloom Energy stick added for morning energy boost.
- Fast, easy, nutrient-dense.
