# Chicken Stir Fry

A quick, savory stir fry with chicken and vegetables.

## Macros (per serving)

- **Calories:** 343
- **Carbohydrates:** 29g
- **Protein:** 26g
- **Fat:** 13g

## Ingredients

- Chicken breast (or thighs), sliced
- Mixed vegetables (bell peppers, broccoli, snap peas, carrots, etc.)
- Stir fry sauce (soy sauce, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, cornstarch slurry)
- Cooking oil
- Optional: rice or noodles as base

## Method

1. Slice chicken into thin strips or bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
3. Cook chicken until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
4. Add vegetables to the pan, stir fry until crisp-tender.
5. Return chicken to the pan, add sauce, toss to coat.
6. Cook until sauce thickens and everything is heated through.
7. Serve over rice or noodles if desired.

## Notes

- Macros calculated without rice/noodles — add those separately if used.
- High protein, moderate carbs, good for post-workout or balanced dinner.
- Quick weeknight meal — done in under 30 minutes.
