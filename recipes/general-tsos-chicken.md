### Air-Fried Version

Uses ~1 Tbsp oil for coating instead of deep-frying.

| Macro | Per Serving |
|-------|-------------|
| Calories | ~330 |
| Protein | ~24g |
| Carbs | ~29g |
| Fat | ~12g |

## Notes

- High-fat dish when deep-fried because of oil absorption. Best as an occasional splurge or on days with more fat budget.
- Air-frying cuts roughly **95–100 calories and 11g of fat per serving** while keeping the protein and carbs about the same.
- For a lighter version, air-fry the coated chicken and use less oil in the sauce.
