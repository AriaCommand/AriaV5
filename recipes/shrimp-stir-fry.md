# Shrimp Stir Fry

## Ingredients (Serves 4)
- 1 lb raw shrimp
- 1/2 onion
- 1 bell pepper
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Macros per Serving
- **Calories:** ~147
- **Protein:** ~28g
- **Carbs:** ~5g
- **Fat:** ~2g

## With Rice (1 cup cooked white rice)
- **Calories:** ~250
- **Carbs:** ~55g
- **Protein:** ~5g
- **Fat:** ~0.5g

## Total per Serving (Stir Fry + Rice)
- **Calories:** ~400
- **Protein:** ~33g
- **Carbs:** ~60g
- **Fat:** ~2.5g

## Notes
- Quick dinner option. Serve with 1 cup cooked white rice.
- Kane's portion: 1 serving of stir fry + 1 cup rice.
