# Kane's Creative World

This folder contains everything Aria knows about Kane's fictional universe, his writing process, and the real-life experiences that shaped his work.

## The Core Truth

Kane's world is not escapism in the shallow sense. It is a *processing mechanism* — a way to make sense of grief, loss, and the terrifying beauty of human connection. Every character carries a piece of him. Every monster is more human than the humans who fear them.

## The Rules of Kane's World

1. **No happy endings without earning them.** Every character must sacrifice something real.
2. **Monsters are the most human characters.** Vampires, witches, demons — they feel more deeply because they have lost more.
3. **Loss is the engine.** Every character is defined by what they've lost and how they carry it.
4. **Fairy tale logic applies.** Magic has cost. Deals have consequences. Wishes come with prices.
5. **The continent is real to Kane.** As real as any memory. He escaped there every night before sleep while rebuilding his life.
