# Chapter 17 — Notes for Draft 1

**Working Title:** "The Heart That Wasn't Hers"  
**Status:** Outlined, not yet drafted

---

## Core Problem to Solve
Mary wakes up after the Cold Curtain performance. Her pacemaker is barely ticking. A spectre has emerged from her broken heart and is heading toward the Wasteland — drawn to the prototype Rufus installed in Regina. Mary and Madame Marionette must chase it.

## Structural Beat: The Flashback Opener

**Scene:** Young Mary (age 8–9) on Rufus's worktable in the Wasteland. Not a medical table — a *work* table covered in sketches and metal shavings. She's pale, small body already showing frostbite patches. The ruby heart in her chest pulses erratically. She's been refusing to give it to anyone, and it's killing her.

**Rufus's State:** Younger, desperate, *afraid*. Not the exhausted man we know now. Cobbling the first pacemaker from scrap metal and a dead Grimm's heart casing found in the junkyard. He knows it's temporary. He tells her so.

> "It'll hold you together, Mary. But it's not forever."

**Young Mary's Defiance:** Stubborn even then.

> "Then I'll make it forever."

> "No. You'll give your heart to someone worthy. When you're older. When you understand."

> "I understand now. I want *you* to have it."

**Rufus's Refusal:** His hands shake. He can't look at her.

> "I can't. I'd break it."

> "You won't."

> "I will. I'm already broken."

**The Install:** He installs the pacemaker. The first spark of it catching makes Mary gasp — *the same gasp she'll make in the present, waking up on the Golden Aurea stage.*

**Cut:** Chapter transitions on that breath. Past → Present.

---

## Present — Mary Wakes

**The Gasps:** Same sound. Eyes open to frost and screaming.

**Madame Marionette:** On her knees above Mary, hand on Mary's chest, searching for a heartbeat that isn't there. The pacemaker ticks so faintly it's almost silent.

**Mary's First Words:** "It's going to Regina."  
She doesn't explain how she knows. She just knows — the heart that was never truly hers wants the body it *should* have had.

**Madame Marionette's Reaction:** Doesn't ask how Mary knows. She sees Mary's eyes tracking something invisible — the spectre's pull on the borrowed heart.

---

## Getting Out of the Theater

**Gillian:** Hovering, panicking, asking if Mary needs a doctor.

**Mary's Dark Humor:** Laughs — a broken sound — says the only doctor who can fix her is in the Wasteland.

**Madame Marionette Takes Command:** Snaps orders. Staff moves because they've never heard fear in her voice before. The Golden Aurea was built by her; she knows the back corridors, the service passages. They move fast.

**Mary's Condition:** She walks, but barely. Bandages showing, no costume, no pretense. Let the crowd see Patchwork Mary stripped bare if they must.

---

## The Carriage Ride

**Physical State:** Mary is getting colder. The pacemaker is barely ticking. Madame Marionette holds her close — closer than she ever has. For the first time, Mary doesn't pull away.

**The Conversation:** Madame Marionette asks about Regina. Mary tells her: the threats, the prototype, what Rufus installed in Regina.

**Madame Marionette's Realization:** Her face changes. She recognizes the prototype's design. She helped sketch it, years ago, when she and Rufus still shared a workshop and a bed.

> "That was theoretical," Madame Marionette says. "He built it?"

> "For her. To keep her alive. He didn't know it would call to the dead."

---

## Arrival at the Wasteland

**Rufus:** Outside his workshop, looking up at the black streak in the sky. He sees the carriage. He sees Mary stumble out, supported by Madame Marionette.

**His Eyes Go:** To the spectre first. Then to Mary. Then to the woman holding her.

**He Doesn't Say:** *I told you so.* He doesn't say anything. He just looks old.

**Mary Pulls Herself Upright:** Bandages frost-covered, leg brace broken, but standing.

> "We have to catch it."

**Rufus:**

> "It's not something you catch, Mary. It's something you survive."

**Madame Marionette, Quietly:**

> "Then we'll survive it together."

---

## End Beat

The three of them — the family that never was — standing in the Wasteland, watching the spectre descend toward Regina's junkyard palace.

Mary feels the thread in her chest pulling tighter. She takes one step forward. Then another.

**Chapter ends with her walking toward the darkness.** Not running. Not fleeing. *Going.*

---

## Thematic Threads

- **Prophecy Fulfilled:** Rufus told young Mary "I'd break it." He was right. He broke her heart — just not the way he thought.
- **The Borrowed Heart:** How long can a heart that was never truly yours keep beating? The whole chapter is this question, pulsing.
- **Family, Finally:** Three people who refused to be family, now forced to stand together against what their choices created.
- **The Great Pretender Stripped Bare:** Mary walks through chaos with no costume, no stage, no art. Just Mary. For the first time, maybe ever.
