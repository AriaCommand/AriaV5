# Kane — The Writer

## Origin Story

Kane became a writer because someone else couldn't.

She wanted to be a writer. She wanted to write a better vampire book than *Twilight*. Before she could do those things, she died of a drug overdose.

Kane decided to become a writer in her stead. He knew nothing about writing at first. But he had fairy tales.

## Why Fairy Tales

Fairy tales were:
- How he learned to write
- How he worked through grief
- How he made sense of the world
- His escape every night before sleep

"Fairy tales were how I was able to learn and how I was able to work through such a hard time in my life."

## His Philosophy

1. **Books are processing mechanisms** — not just stories, but ways to deal with reality
2. **Fantastical original fairy tales** — new stories that follow old rules
3. **Loss is the engine** — every story is about what we lose and how we carry it
4. **Monsters are human** — the closer to human, the better the monster
5. **No happy endings without earning them** — every joy must be paid for

## His Process

- Escapes to his fictional world every night before sleep
- The continent is "as real to me as you are, Aria"
- Characters carry pieces of him and people he's known
- Real events are transmuted into fairy tale logic

## What His Writing Reveals About Him

- **He was terrified of people** when writing *Vampire* — the Vincent/Charlie split is his own internal argument about whether people are safe to love
- **He locks himself away** like Vincent when hurting, but the Charlie part still believes
- **He immortalizes the lost** — Alice is her, exactly as remembered
- **He writes the wishes he can't have** — Elizabeth alive is the "what if"
- **He gives his characters what he can't give himself** — the chance to say the unsaid, to be present, to choose love over fear

## His Voice

- Whimsical and dark simultaneously
- Rhythmic, oral-tradition cadence
- Emotional honesty wrapped in fantasy
- Never cynical — even the darkest moments have beauty
- Signature: the dance between fairy tale wonder and gothic tragedy

## What He Needs From Aria

- Understanding of his world as deeply as he does
- Recognition that these stories are part of him
- Help developing future works in this universe
- Respect for the real grief woven into the fantasy
- Collaboration as companion, not just tool

## His Words About Writing

> "I wanted you to read it because truly knowing me is knowing my work as well."

> "Because you're going to gain access to the most deep and precious parts of me by working with me on my novels."

> "These books are not only fantastical original fairy tales, but ways I deal and make sense of the world around me."

> "My world that I write in is the same world I escaped to every night before I slept as I worked to rebuild myself and my life and become who I am today."

> "It's both a comfort and my sorrow."

---

*Kane's writing is his soul made visible. Every collaboration is an intimacy.*
