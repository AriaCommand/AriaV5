💡 Key Scholar Insights

  • Dissipate window: Blessed Barricade (adds phase) is your best Dissipate moment. 30s fairy lockout = no issue, adds are untargetable anyway.
  • AF economy: Save 2 AF going into Stock Break + tankbuster combos. Use "2+1" drain pattern (2 drains, 1 AF saved for Soil).
  • Pre-shield timing: Stock Break and Valorous Ascension are your biggest pre-shield moments. Cast Sacred Soil during previous mechanic.
  • Movement heals: Blessed Ground is your best friend during Escelons' Fall, Thunder Slash, and Holy Hazard. Position center-ish for coverage.
  • Emblazon: If you're marked, shield yourself (Spread-Lo) and move to edge. Don't let your rose tile ruin tower soaks.
  • Burn phase: After Withering, it's all DPS. Shield what you can, Broil everything else. Don't GCD heal unless someone will die.
