# Hisuian Zoroark — VGC Analysis

**Type:** Normal/Ghost
**Ability:** Illusion (appears as last party Pokémon)
**Base Stats:** 55/100/60/125/60/110

---

## Key Strengths
- **Illusion** — mind games, disguise as threatening teammate
- **Bitter Malice** — 75 BP Ghost, lowers Attack (signature)
- **Shadow Ball** — STAB special
- **Hyper Voice** — STAB Normal, bypasses Substitute
- **Nasty Plot** — +2 SpA setup
- **Good speed tier** — 110 base outspeeds a lot

---

## Common Roles

### Disruptor/Attacker
- Illusion bluff as physical threat
- Bitter Malice to cripple physical attackers
- Shadow Ball/Hyper Voice for damage

### Choice Scarf User
- Trick opponent with Illusion
- U-Turn pivot (if available)
- Fast Bitter Malice spam

### Setup Sweeper
- Nasty Plot behind Illusion disguise
- Requires good team support

---

## M-C Format Considerations

### New Threats
- **Rillaboom** — Grassy Terrain weakens Earthquake, but Fake Out pressure
- **Indeedee-F** — Psychic Terrain blocks priority, can redirect
- **Pawmot** — Revival Blessing punishes your KOs
- **Mega Salamence** — Aerilate Extreme Speed/Return hits hard

### New Opportunities
- **Terrain Seeds** — could enable Unburden shenanigans
- **More Megas** — Illusion disguises as Mega = extra mind games
- **Rocky Helmet** — punishes contact physical attackers

---

## Team Synergy Ideas

**Good Disguise Partners:**
- Physical attackers (make them think you're weak to Fairy/Fighting)
- Steel types (lure Fire/Fighting moves you resist)
- Other Ghosts (bluff spinblocker)

**Support Needs:**
- Speed control (Tailwind/Trick Room)
- Fake Out pressure to enable setup
- Redirection to protect the illusion
