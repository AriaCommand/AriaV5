# Pokémon Champions — M-C Format Notes

**Active:** September 9 – December 2, 2026
**Platform:** Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android

---

## Core Rules (VGC Standard)
- **Format:** Double Battles (4v4 from 6-Pokémon team)
- **Timers:** Team preview 90s | Move 45s | Player time 7min | Game 20min
- **Level:** Auto-level 50
- **Clauses:** Species Clause, Item Clause (no duplicates)
