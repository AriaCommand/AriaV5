## Core Concept: Hisuian Zoroark + Weather/Terrain Control

---

## Slot 1: Hisuian Zoroark (Anchor)

**Role:** Special Attacker / Disruptor / Hex Cleaner

| Item | Ability | Nature | EVs |
|------|---------|--------|-----|
| Life Orb | Illusion | Timid | 4 HP / 252 SpA / 252 Spe |

**Moves:**
- **Hex** — 130 BP on statused targets (the finisher)
- **Bitter Malice** — 75 BP Ghost, drops Attack (cripples physical threats)
- **Shadow Ball** — Reliable STAB when no status
- **Protect** — Standard doubles

**Why Here:** Zoroark cleans up what the team paralyzes/burns/puts to sleep. Illusion provides free information and mind games.
